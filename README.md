# Tiers

A simple todo app with your own priority tiers, a calendar, and a backlog.

- Plan your day by tier
- Keep everything else in a backlog
- Tap **Suggest from backlog** and AI picks what to do next
- Sign in with Google to sync your computer and phone

## Download

Get the latest version from [Releases](../../releases):

- **Mac:** `Tiers_x.x.x_universal.dmg`
- **Windows:** `Tiers_x.x.x_x64-setup.exe`

The first time you open it:

- **Mac:** right-click the app and choose **Open**
- **Windows:** click **More info**, then **Run anyway**

After that it updates itself.

## iPhone

Open **https://pizza-boy101.github.io/tiers/** in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

## Sync

In **Settings**, click **Sign in with Google** on each device using the same account.

## Suggestions

Free with Gemini once you're signed in (25 a day), or use your own Claude API key in **Settings**.
